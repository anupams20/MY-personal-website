Questions

The things every doctor asks.

I don't have content or photos. Is that a problem?

No — it's the normal situation. I write all the text for your specialty, use your Google photos and reviews where they exist, and you can WhatsApp me 10 photos from your phone for the rest. Real photos of your actual clinic always build more trust than stock images.

I'm already on Practo/Justdial. Why do I need my own website?

Those platforms rent you visibility — next to ads for your competitors, on their terms, with their branding. Your own website is an asset you own: it ranks on Google for your name and specialty, carries your reviews, and sends patients to YOUR WhatsApp — not into a marketplace that also lists the clinic across the road.

Can my receptionist really use the OPD software?

Yes — it's deliberately simple: search patient, add visit, print prescription. I train your front desk on a call when we go live (most people are comfortable within a day), and I remain available on WhatsApp if anything confuses them.

Is this allowed under medical advertising rules?

Yes — if it's done right, and most local web designers don't know the rules. The NMC guidelines (updated 2026) prohibit patient testimonials, "best doctor" claims, discount offers and guaranteed cures on a doctor's website — but fully permit factual information: qualifications, departments, services, facilities, timings and charges. I build every clinic site to be NMC-compliant from day one, so your website builds your practice without risking your registration.

Why are you cheaper than agencies quoting ₹80,000?

No office rent, no sales team, no project manager forwarding emails. You pay one engineer for the hours he works — and because I only build for healthcare, I reuse a solid, tested foundation instead of starting from scratch each time. Less work, same quality, lower price.

Who owns the domain and the data?

You do. Domain in your name, logins handed over, patient data stays on YOUR computer (the OPD software works offline with local backups — nothing is uploaded anywhere without your say-so). You're never locked in to me.

How do payments work?

You approve a working demo of your own website first. Then payment by UPI or bank transfer — for larger packages we can split it, part on approval and part on go-live. If the demo isn't right, you walk away having paid nothing.

My clinic isn't in UP or Hyderabad. Can you still help?

Yes — I work with clinics anywhere in India. Everything happens over WhatsApp, email and calls. Most of my current clients are in cities I've never visited — it makes no difference to what you get.
