NMC rules (updated 2026) — what your website can and cannot say.

✅ Allowed — and enough

  • Your qualifications and registration
  • Departments and services
  • Timings and address
  • Fees, if you want
  • Photos of your clinic
  • Appointment booking

❌ Not allowed — I never add these

  • Patient testimonials
  • "Best doctor" claims
  • Discounts and offers
  • Guaranteed cure promises
  • Before/after patient photos

Final responsibility for content on a doctor's site stays with the doctor; I follow the current guidelines when building.

Source: the National Medical Commission's professional-conduct regulations — read them yourself: nmc.org.in ↗

Why most local designers get this wrong

They copy shop-website templates — testimonial sliders, "50% off" banners. On a doctor's website those can invite NMC trouble. I build only for clinics, so every page follows the rules from day one.
