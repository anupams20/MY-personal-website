Privacy, in plain words.

Short version: almost nothing is collected, nothing is sold.

What this website collects

Browsing: an anonymous visit counter (how many people visited, which buttons were tapped). No names, no phone numbers, no cookies banner needed — there is nothing personal in it.
हिंदी: सिर्फ़ anonymous गिनती — कितने लोग आए। कोई नाम या नंबर नहीं।

The quiz: your score is calculated on your phone. Nothing is sent unless you fill the small form at the end — then only the name, number and clinic you type are saved, so I can send your checklist and (if you ask) a demo.
हिंदी: Quiz का score आपके phone पर ही बनता है। Form भरेंगे तभी नाम-नंबर मुझ तक आएगा।

The calculator and clinic-preview tool: run fully on your phone. Nothing is sent anywhere until you tap WhatsApp.
हिंदी: Calculator और preview आपके phone पर चलते हैं। WhatsApp दबाने तक कुछ नहीं भेजा जाता।

WhatsApp and calls

Tapping a WhatsApp button opens YOUR WhatsApp with a ready message — you see it and you decide to send it or not. Calls are normal phone calls. WhatsApp's own privacy policy applies to messages you send there.

My promise

I never sell or share any data. Anything you send me (name, number, clinic details) is used only to build and discuss your website. Ask me to delete it anytime: WhatsApp or email.
हिंदी: कोई data कभी बेचा या बाँटा नहीं जाता। जब चाहें delete करवा लीजिए।
