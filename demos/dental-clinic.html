Before you come

Common questions

Will the treatment hurt?
Local anaesthetic is used for every procedure that needs it, including root canals. Most patients tell us afterwards that the injection was the worst part. If you are anxious, say so when booking and we will give you a longer slot.
Do you accept insurance and EMI?
All major insurance providers are accepted, and we handle the paperwork. EMI is available on any treatment above ₹15,000 through most credit and debit cards.
How much will my treatment cost?
Prices above are the starting cost for each treatment. After your check-up you get the exact figure in writing, and it does not change afterwards. You can also send a photo on WhatsApp for an estimate before visiting.
Can I get an appointment today?
Usually yes. Slots are kept free each day for emergencies and same-day requests. Call or WhatsApp and we will tell you honestly whether today is possible.
My child is scared of the dentist. What do you do?
Dr. Kavya sees children in a separate room kept for them. The first visit is usually just a look and a chat, with no treatment at all, so the child learns the place is not frightening.
