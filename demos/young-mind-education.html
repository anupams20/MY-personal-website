Questions

Questions parents ask

My child has never been to school. Will they settle?

Most do within two weeks. The first few days are short, a parent may wait nearby, and we call you the same afternoon to tell you how it went.

Can I visit before admission?

Yes, and you should. Come any working day, no appointment needed. The first visit is free and you are welcome to sit in a class.

Do you take children from other schools for coaching?

Yes. After-school coaching is open to children from any school — homework support and focused help in weak subjects.

Is it English medium?

Yes, with special focus on Science and Maths. Teachers speak to the younger children in whichever language helps them settle.

How do I know what happened during the day?

Updates and photos go to parents on WhatsApp, and any concern is a phone call the same day rather than a note at the end of term.
