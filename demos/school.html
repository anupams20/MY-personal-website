From the Principal

A message to parents

RM
A school is not its building. It is what a child becomes in it. We have kept our classes at thirty-two students for twenty-eight years, not because it is easy, but because a teacher who knows every name teaches differently from one who does not.

We publish our results in full, including the students who did not clear. We publish our fees in full, so no parent is surprised later. Admissions 2027–28

How to apply

Registration is open from 1 November. Forms are issued at the school office and online.

  1. EnquireSend us a message on WhatsApp or call the office. We will tell you honestly whether seats are open in your child's class.
  2. Visit the campusWalk through with a coordinator, any working day between 9 AM and 1 PM. No appointment needed.
  3. Submit the formBirth certificate, Aadhaar, previous report card and four photographs. Form fee ₹500, non-refundable.
  4. InteractionAn informal session with the child, and a short meeting with parents. Nursery to Class II is interaction only — no written test.

For parents

Questions we are asked most

Is there an entrance test for admission?
Not for Nursery to Class II — those are an informal interaction with the child and a short meeting with parents. From Class III upward there is a written assessment in English, Hindi and Mathematics at the level of the previous class.
Do you charge a capitation or donation fee?
No. The admission fee and tuition published on this page are the only charges, apart from optional transport. If anyone asks you for anything else at any stage, please tell the Principal directly.
Are seats available in the middle of the session?
Sometimes, when a family transfers out. Call the office and we will tell you honestly rather than making you fill a form first.
What is the transport fee?
It depends on the route and is charged quarterly, from ₹3,200 to ₹5,400. The office will give you the exact figure for your address.
Can I see the school before applying?
Yes, and we would rather you did. Walk in any working day between 9 AM and 1 PM. No appointment is needed and nobody will pressure you.
