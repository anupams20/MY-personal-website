Questions

Questions we are asked most

Can my child join in the middle of the session?

Yes, if the batch has a seat. The teacher gives two extra Sunday sessions free to cover what has already been taught.

Do you teach UP Board as well as CBSE?

Both. The Classes 9–10 and Commerce batches are mixed; the teacher covers the extra CBSE topics in the last twenty minutes.

What if my child misses a class?

Tell us on WhatsApp the same morning. The notes go to you that evening, and the teacher repeats the topic in the Sunday doubt hour.

Are there tests?

Every Saturday, and a full paper each month. Marks go to the parent on WhatsApp, not only to the child.

Is there a demo class fee?

No. The first class is free and you owe nothing if you decide against joining.
