Our story

We buy from fourteen weaving families. That's the whole supply chain.

Kalika started in 2019 because good handloom was either impossible to find or priced like a luxury import. We work directly with weavers in Pochampally, Gadwal and Narayanpet, pay upfront before the loom starts, and sell at a fixed margin.

Every saree carries the name of the person who wove it. If you want to know how long yours took, ask us — we'll tell you.
